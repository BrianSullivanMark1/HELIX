# HELIX - what changed on 2026-09-22

One page for the day, kept as the day went on. Everything here is on branch `mark1`. Nothing
below claims a run it did not have: the sandbox ran the Python tests (314 green) and the web
build; the PC-side checks are listed at the bottom as yours.

## CURRENT TASKS - the register

* `helix/services/jobs.py` - every long thing is a **Job**: kind, a title in plain words, app/env/by,
  state (running / done / failed / cancelled), progress 0..1 or unknown, the current note, its
  lines (ASCII-cleaned, capped at 600), and a **declared cancel**: a hook plus one sentence saying
  what stopping leaves behind. No sentence = cannot be stopped (a rollback, a fleet read).
* Kept on disk as `data/jobs.json` (last 200). After a restart finished work is still there;
  anything that was running comes back **failed** with "HELIX restarted while this was running".
* Pushed live to the page: `{"t":"job"}` (throttled to ~6/s per job), `{"t":"job_line"}`, `{"t":"job_gone"}`.
* `helix/api/jobs_routes.py`: `GET /api/jobs`, `GET /api/jobs/{id}` (with lines), `POST /api/jobs/{id}/cancel`,
  `DELETE /api/jobs/{id}` (dismiss, finished only), `POST /api/jobs/clear`, and three routes for
  page-driven upload tasks (`POST /api/jobs`, `PUT .../progress`, `POST .../finish`).
* Wired in: deploys and rollbacks (`services/deploy.py` - a deploy's cancel kills the PowerShell
  tree with `taskkill /T /F`; Cloud Run keeps the old revision serving), fleet reads and secrets
  scans (`api/fleet_routes.py`), merge checks (`api/deploy_routes.py`), uploads, fetches and
  caching the station (`services/radio.py`, `api/radio_routes.py`).
* `helix/app/webboot.py`: closing the last window while a task runs no longer quits HELIX; it
  checks every 10 s and quits once the register is quiet (the browser also warns on close).
* Page: `web/src/lib/jobs.ts` (the store; the moment a task ends = chime + lattice wave + toast),
  `web/src/lib/chime.ts` (synthesised sounds: done, failed, start; `helix_sounds=off` in
  localStorage mutes), `web/src/components/Tasks.tsx` + `tasks.css` (the **section above PROJECTS**,
  the **dock** at the bottom of every page - fold/unfold, drag the top edge, height remembered -
  the **log window** with Copy log / Copy for debugging, the Stop question with the sentence,
  Dismiss, Clear finished, toasts).

## The Strandbar - one progress bar

* `web/src/components/Strandbar.tsx` + `strandbar.css`: a helix being sequenced. Rungs light as the
  work advances, a bead rides the tip, unknown length = a reading window sweeping the strand;
  done = a gold sweep with sparks; failed = ember, cracked in three places, a shake; stopped = grey.
  Small words cycle under it (sequencing, transcribing, translating, folding, annealing, splicing,
  replicating, ligating, proofreading), then SEQUENCED / BROKEN / STOPPED. Canvas at device pixels,
  stops drawing when still. Used by the tasks, the dock, the log window and the radio upload.

## The radio

* **The DJ is the real face** (`Organism` in `mode="dj"` inside the stage): head-bang on the kick,
  groove on the half beat, a shrug now and then, a happy set (grin, eyes-shut groove, delight,
  cheeky tongue, wink), the mouse works its lattice inside the stage, clicks send waves.
* **Music video performance**: on a fresh start the DJ winks, zooms off toward you, a
  **neural curtain** knits over the stage and falls into the picture (the song starts under it);
  on resume the DJ peeks in from the side, nods, and the picture ripples back. `NeuralCurtain`
  in `Radio.tsx`; cues ride `helix-dj` events.
* **Shelves** (folders): in the catalog for everyone; four deep; drag a track onto a shelf, or the
  "Move to a shelf" select on the row; rename; remove only when empty. Backend:
  `create_folder / rename_folder / remove_folder / move_track / retitle` in `services/radio.py`;
  routes `POST/PUT/DELETE /api/radio/folder`, `PUT /api/radio/track`.
* **Upload with real progress**: `PUT /api/radio/upload?name=&title=&artist=&theme=&bpm=&folder=`,
  the file as the body. Half one = bytes arriving at HELIX; half two = a **resumable HTTPS upload**
  to the bucket with the user's own gcloud access token, 8 MB pieces (`adapters/gcs_radio.py`).
  A task from the first byte; Stop leaves nothing in the catalog. The old multipart route stays.
* **Faster media, no consoles**: `GET /api/radio/play/{id}` streams a file down over HTTPS into the
  cache and serves it **while it arrives**; cached files serve with Range (seek). The next track
  prefetches. **Cache the whole station** is a task (the gear). Cap in GB (`radio_cache_gb`,
  5 default), oldest-played goes first. `gcloud` is only used for the catalog now.
* Tabs restyled (Tracks with a count, Upload, a visible glowing gear).

## The face, the lattice, the helices

* Talk's lattice is **3D**: more than half the nodes crowd the face's rim and thin out with
  distance; near ones are bigger, brighter, their links brighter; the shell breathes and
  **follows the head** when it moves (`NeuralLayer` gained `depth` and `follow`).
* Helices turn about their long axis faster, faster still in the background (`spinRate`), and
  **breathe** slow-fast-slow over ~14 s; the strands' travel is integrated (no jumps) and the
  music rides on the breath instead of driving it flat out; a kick gives one shove.
* The wordmark is a **canvas** helix turning about its axis at device pixels - crisp; the SVG
  with blur filters is gone.
* The docked face **works**: think / scan / hmm acts, faster and with a light pulse when HELIX
  is busy or a task runs.
* **The stage**: click the docked face and it leaps to the front; the page is pushed back
  (blur, dim, a hue shift), the head roams a slow figure-eight up close, the lattice's halo
  follows it, the helices spin harder. Talk to me / Back to work / Esc / click outside.
* A **wink** uniform (`uWink`) in the face shader.

## Evening round - performance first (Brian: "slow as ass")

What was eating the frames, and what changed:

* **Blur behind moving art.** Every panel with `backdrop-filter: blur()` (the app cards, the
  glass pills, tiles, the dock, the rail, the radio and drawer overlays, toasts, settings) had to
  re-blur the animating helix and lattice behind it on every frame. All 19 blurs are gone; the
  panels are a touch more opaque instead. Same for CSS `filter: drop-shadow` on things that
  redraw every frame (the docked face's canvas, the shimmering CONSOLE title, the radio logo).
* **Pixels.** Full-window 2D layers (lattice, backdrop, sparks, the code curtain) were drawn at 2x
  device pixels - four times the work on a HiDPI screen. Capped at 1.25x. The 3D faces are capped
  at 1.5x (1.25x for the docked head) instead of 2x.
* **The governor** (`web/src/lib/perf.ts`): measures the real frame time; in Auto it steps the art
  down (full -> lean -> minimal) after ~2 s of long frames and back up after ~20 s of short ones.
  Lean = 1.25x pixels, a thinner lattice, no glow halos, 2D at 30 fps; minimal = 1x, half the
  lattice, no backdrop scene, one far helix fewer, 2D at 20 fps. Settings > Voice & look >
  **Performance** (Auto / Full / Lean / Minimal); the menu's build line shows the live fps and level.
* **Loops on a budget.** The twenty status faces, the helix marks, the strand bars and the lattice
  run on the governor's 2D budget and stop while off screen (faces) or hidden (all).
* **Blur in canvases.** `shadowBlur` per point/segment was the cost of the wordmark (128 blurred
  fills a frame) and of every strand bar (~200): the mark draws pre-rendered glow sprites; a bar
  draws one wide faint stroke under its lit part.
* **The helix bend.** The far strands on Talk never bend under the mouse (3,600 projections a
  frame nobody could see); the near one bends every 2nd/3rd frame when lean/minimal.
* **Fewer contexts.** The docked head is unmounted, not hidden, while the radio deck or a log
  window is open. The face's code texture is smaller and re-rolls less often. The sphere is 96
  segments (was 128). The finished code curtain leaves the compositor (display: none).
* **Fewer re-renders.** The Console subscribes to the task counts through a shallow selector, so an
  upload's six progress ticks a second no longer re-render the whole page.
* Boot curtain minimum 1.4 s (was 2.2).

Also this round: **git strands** - the lines between commits are small double helices that turn
and go liquid under the mouse (`GitStrands`, a canvas under the SVG nodes).

Not measured on your PC (the sandbox has no GPU; its numbers are all software rendering). The
menu's fps line is the measurement: send me the number on Console and on Talk.
* Late: the stage face pulled back to Talk's distance and a modest roam in the upper two thirds

  The fps readout is a switch in Settings > Overview ("Show the frame rate") that puts a small

* **Power off** in the ☰ menu (below HELIX radio): an Are-you-sure card (it says how many tasks
  would be cut off), then the boot curtain in reverse - the helix unwinds into dust from the top
  down, the ring opens, the word decodes into noise, POWERING DOWN, GOOD NIGHT - and only when it
  is dark does HELIX get the quit (`POST /api/shell/quit`), then the HELIX IS OFF screen.
  `components/Shutdown.tsx` + `shutdown.css`.

  the pills; the grep; the four conditions; the routes echo no value). 326 green.

  gradient overlay); only the glow behind them breathes, and swells on a kick.
